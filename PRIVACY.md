# Privacy

**All processing happens in your browser. No transaction or company data leaves your device.**

- The app has no server, database, login or cloud storage.
- Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere. There is no analytics, telemetry, cookie tracking, remote logging or error reporting.
- No third-party fonts, scripts or CDNs are loaded at runtime — everything is bundled with the app.
- **Company Master** values (legal name, GSTIN, address) are saved only in this browser's `localStorage` when you click *Save company data*. They stay on this device and this browser.
- The **challan register** (list of used challan numbers, for uniqueness checks) is likewise stored only in `localStorage`.
- Generated Word / Excel / JSON files are created in the browser and downloaded straight to your machine.

## Clearing your data

- **Clear saved company data** — removes only the Company Master from `localStorage`.
- **Reset entire app** — removes the Company Master, any saved draft and the challan register.

You can also clear the site's data from your browser settings at any time.

## What this tool is not

This is a compliance-assistance tool. It does not submit anything to the GST or e-way bill portal, does not calculate tax, and is not a substitute for transaction-specific advice from your GST reviewer.
